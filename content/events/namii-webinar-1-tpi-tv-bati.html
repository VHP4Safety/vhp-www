Twelve NAM information initiatives – together under the name NAMii – have joined forces to organise an inspiring webinar series. Find out how you can use these knowledge bases, search engines, infrastructures, and other tools to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of your NAM-based research!

The first webinar will be on the video platform TPI.tv and the Beyond Animal Testing Index. More information and the registration link can be found here: NAMii Webinar Series | ZonMw.
