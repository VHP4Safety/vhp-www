VHP4Safety's first live Hackathon
Sign up for the very first live VHP4Safety Hackathon! During this event we will work together on building the Virtual Human Platform. People from all Research Lines as well as cofunders and collaboration partners are welcome to attend. Is programming not quite your thing? No worries, that doesn't matter. Different skills are welcome and needed during our Hackathon. We highly encourage (young) researchers from the case studies, the ADME studies and experts on risk assessment to participate in this event. Together, we'll make it a great co-creation day. Interested to join? Please send an email to VHP4safety@uu.nl
