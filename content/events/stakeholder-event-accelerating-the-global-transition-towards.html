The following save-the-date was sent to us by the AFARA project:

As a learning community, we invite you to this event to explore with us what is needed to further accelerate the international adoption of animal-free testing models. For this, we bring together the expertise of three distinctive transdisciplinary research consortia: AFARA, SAFE and Valuing Testing. Each consortium offers unique perspectives on the transition to animal-free science and innovation, reflecting their expertise in toxicology, social sciences, humanities, regulation and practice.

The global scientific community is decisively moving from animal experimentation to using more human-relevant New Approach Methodologies (NAMs). The transition toward animal free innovation appears to be entering an accelerated phase. What is driving this acceleration? And how can we, as a community, contribute to meaningful, global progress?

During this gathering, we will explore:

  • Emerging developments and breakthroughs shaping the transition
  • How AFARA, SAFE, and Valuing Testing contribute to the acceleration
  • How innovative, participatory methods from the projects can help interpret transition dynamics and facilitate the discussion with stakeholders

This meeting will bring together key stakeholders to reflect, align, and identify opportunities for collective action.

ZonMw – The Dutch organisation for knowledge and innovation in health, healthcare and wellbeing and TPI – Transition Programme for animal-free Innovation, warmly invite you to this stakeholder event hosted by the AFARA, SAFE, and Valuing Testing consortia, part of the NWA programme Animal Free Assessment Models: Acceptance and Implementation.

A formal invitation with programme details will follow.

Date: Wednesday 13 May 2026

Time: 12:00 – 17:00, followed by drinks

Location: Veegenszaal Bezuidenhoutseweg 73, Den Haag (EZ/KGG/LVVN)
