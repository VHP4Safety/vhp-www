On 6 and 7 February 2025, the 4th VHP4Safety Hackathon will be held. We are happy to share with you that the hackathon will take place under the slogan:

“Using and expanding the first version of the VHP4Safety platform”

During the hackathon we will present the first version of the user-interface that integrates a few software components to perform a risk assessment task. We aim for the individual teams to expand on that integration with tools, data and models that they use in their research.

Of course, we will be connecting all the hackathon work to the project case studies and will be mapping the VHP4Safety modules to the case study workflows.

We are looking forward to seeing you there!
