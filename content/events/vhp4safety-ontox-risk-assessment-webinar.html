We're excited to announce the final ONTOX-VHP4Safety webinar, titled "Strategies for Next-Generation Risk Assessment in ONTOX and VHP4Safety"!

Join us on December 18 at 15:00 CET for a session that explores how both projects translate data into decision making to support next-generation chemical safety evaluation. Early-stage researchers from both consortia will present their work, with contributions from Trine Husøy, Susana Proença, Julen Sanz Serrano and Fabian Wagenaars.

Register via the following link: Webinar Registration - Zoom.

This webinar concludes a larger series co-organised with the ASPIS Academy, highlighting collaboration between ONTOX and VHP4Safety and showcasing the work of young scientists across both projects.

You can rewatch the first four webinars on the ASPIS Cluster YouTube channel: ONTOX-VHP4Safety Early-stage researchers webinar 1.

This session is open to everyone, and the recording will be shared online afterwards.
