On June 19, 2024, the 3Rs Centre Utrecht (3RCU) and the Transition to Animal-free Innovations (TPI) programme are organising, together with TPI-Utrecht and the Netherlands National Committee for the protection of animals used for scientific purposes (NCad), a conference at Jaarbeurs Media Plaza Utrecht. The conference aims to connect NAMs to the 3Rs framework, exploring the best way forward for human-based biomedical basic and translational science.

More information can be found here: The 3Rs and NAMs: all-inclusive? - News & Events - Utrecht University (uu.nl).
