How can in vitro research contribute to the Parkinson’s case study?

Join us for the second webinar in our VHP4Safety webinar series, where we will explore the Parkinson’s case study and show how in vitro researchers can share their research through the Virtual Human Platform.

Dr Harm Heusinkveld will introduce the case study, explaining why pesticides are being studied in relation to neurodegeneration and providing some background on this field of research.

Julia Meerman will then present how the Adverse Outcome Pathway was used to design experiments and generate data.

Finally, Jente Houweling will demonstrate how in vitro researchers can add their research to the platform and make use of the methods available there. She will also show a tool that can be used to document in vitro research.

📅 28 October 2026
🕐 13:00 to 14:30 CET

Whether you work with in vitro models or are interested in how in vitro research contributes to the Parkinson’s case study, this webinar offers an opportunity to learn more about the research and the role of the Virtual Human Platform.

This webinar is part of the VHP4Safety webinar series. Join us also on 14 October for an introduction to the Virtual Human Platform and on 18 November to learn more about the social aspects of building the VHP.
