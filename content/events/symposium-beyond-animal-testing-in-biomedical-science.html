On the afternoon of Friday, September 27, during Utrecht Science Week, the symposium Beyond Animal Testing in Biomedical Science will take place. This event is organized at the Institute for Life Sciences & Chemistry (ILC) of Hogeschool Utrecht in collaboration with UU and UMCU.

For more information, visit Beyond Animal Testing in Biomedical Science | Hogeschool Utrecht (hu.nl).
