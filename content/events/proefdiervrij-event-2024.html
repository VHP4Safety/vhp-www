Get on board the scientific train to the future and visit the Proefdiervrij Event 2024, organized by Stichting Proefdiervrij.

Wednesday 24 April is the day: it is World day for laboratory animals and on that day the Proefdiervrij Event will take place in Amare - The Hague. During the event, we will join forces to promote a world free from animal testing. We are all working hard on the transition. We therefore think it is time to reflect on the successes we have already booked, but we also want to look at what goals we want to achieve in the coming years. In short: which milestones are we going to celebrate?
As icing on the cake, the winner of the Proefdiervrij Venture Challenge will be announced.

Tickets can be booked (for free) via: Proefdiervrij Event 2024 Tickets, woe, 24 apr. 2024 om 14:00 | Eventbrite.
