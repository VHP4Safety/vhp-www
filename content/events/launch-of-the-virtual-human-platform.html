On the afternoon of Thursday 28 May, the official launch of the Virtual Human Platform (VHP) will take place, where innovation meets practical application in human-relevant safety assessment. This event promises a dynamic program with something for everyone, including:

  • Three parallel interactive sessions, covering:
    o Data, Models & AI: Combining Computational Tools to Shape the Future of Chemical Safety Assessment
    o From Petri Dish to Platform: How In Vitro Work Contributes to the VHP
    o Breaking Barriers: Overcoming Challenges in Adopting Animal-Free Safety Standards
  • The VHP Info Market, offering the perfect opportunity to network, explore the platform’s tools, and engage with experts.

Registration for this event is free of charge and can be done via the following link: Registration Virtual Human Platform Launch.

We look forward to welcoming you in Utrecht on 28 May. In the meantime, you can explore the platform at https://platform.vhp4safety.nl.
