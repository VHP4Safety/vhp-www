The 14th Gordon Conference on Environmental Endocrine Disruptors (EDs) will be held at the Renaissance Tuscany Il Ciocco Resort in Tuscany, Italy. This conference addresses the role of environmental EDs in planetary health, the transdisciplinary field that addresses the impacts of human activity on the health of humans and all life on Earth.

For more information: 2024 Environmental Endocrine Disruptors Conference GRC.
