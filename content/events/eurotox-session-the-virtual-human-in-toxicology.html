Join our EUROTOX 2025 session "The Virtual Human in Toxicology" on Tuesday 16 September from 14:00–15:30!

Chaired by Anne Kienhuis (RIVM) and Chris Evelo (Maastricht University)
With talks from:
– Chris Evelo (VHP4Safety)
– Holly Mortensen (EPA)
– Andrew White (Unilever)

Next generation risk assessment (NGRA) and the integration of new approach methodologies (NAMs) with data science has unlocked the potential of creating a virtual human that can be used to predict toxicity. This session will present the state-of-the-art of innovative, international initiatives to build virtual human platforms and related NGRA approaches for physiology-based predictions of the human health risks of chemical exposure, such as the ONTOX and VHP4Safety projects. The session describes the technological basis needed to integrate software and data to make predictions on toxicity, including important aspects of data quality. The session provides examples of new approaches to data and model integration for human relevant safety assessments. The virtual human will become an inevitable part of the toxicology of the future; this session will present the opportunities and challenges that its implementation will pose.
