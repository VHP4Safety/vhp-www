oin us for the second VHP4Safety-ONTOX collaboration webinar!

On Tuesday, April 8, from 15:00-16:30 CET, we will explore how stakeholder involvement can support the transition to animal-free risk assessment. How can we ensure that scientific innovations align with societal needs and expectations?

In this session, Michael Guy Diemar (ONTOX), together with Aniek de Winter and Isaac Arturo Ortega Alvarado (VHP4Safety), will discuss how engaging stakeholders can bridge the gap between science and real-world implementation.

Themed “Bridging Science and Society: Stakeholder Involvement in Animal-Free Risk Assessment”, this webinar is open to all, and a certificate of attendance is available upon request.

Looking forward to seeing you there!
