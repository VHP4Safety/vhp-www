On Tuesday October 8th, from 13:00 to 17:00, the NVT section of Risk Assessment will organize their autumn symposium. TNO Utrecht will host the session which can only be
attended in person. During this symposium different methodologies and models for the analysis and management of uncertainties in risk assessment will be presented and discussed together with RIVM, EFSA and KWR. The NVT members are looking forward to inspiring discussions and hope to welcome many of you.

Location: TNO, Princetonlaan 6, 3584 CB Utrecht

Preliminary Program

13.00 – 13.20 Registration
13:20 – 16:30 Welcome
Introduction to probabilistic risk assessment – RIVM
APROBA-plus model – RIVM
Probabilistic risk assessment in a drinking water quality context – a Simazine case study using the APROBA-plus tool – KWR
Expert Knowledge Elicitation (EKE) with applications by EFSA – Lund University
16.30 – 17.00 Closure, drinks & networking

You can register for this meeting by sending an email to Sabine Jetten (sabine.jetten@tno.nl).

Email subject: “Registration NVT autumn symposium – October 8th” and report your name, affiliation and NVT membership (Y/N*) in the email itself.
Registration closes on Monday September 30th, 2024.


*NVT-members have priority during registration.
