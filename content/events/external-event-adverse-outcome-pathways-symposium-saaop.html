We would like to draw your attention to this symposium, which might be of interest to some of the VHP4Safety partcipants.

The 1st AOP Community of Practice symposium, organized by the SAAOP (The Society for Advancement of Adverse Outcome Pathways) is a free 3-day event taking place online on Februari 15, 17 and 22, 2022. More than 200 people have already registered and it is promising to be a very exciting opportunity to learn, and to share experiences on AOP development and use.

The tentative program is available here: https://virtual.oxfordabstracts.com/#/event/2637/program.

The deadline for abstract submission (https://app.oxfordabstracts.com/stages/3344/submitter) is December 3. You can register for the event at https://app.oxfordabstracts.com/events/2637/registrations/new.

Feel free to forward this information to anyone who might be interested!
