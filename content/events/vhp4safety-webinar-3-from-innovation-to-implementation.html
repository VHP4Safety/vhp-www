How can stakeholder perspectives help move the Virtual Human Platform from innovation to implementation?


Join us for the final webinar in our VHP4Safety webinar series, where we will explore the social side of building the Virtual Human Platform and the role of stakeholder perspectives in its development and implementation.

Dr Isaac Ortega Alvarado will present user research supporting the development and implementation of the Virtual Human Platform. Dr Tom Jansen will then discuss public acceptance of computational models and artificial intelligence in risk assessment.

Together, the speakers will show how understanding the needs and perspectives of stakeholders can help shape tools that are scientifically robust, transparent and fit for purpose.

📅 18 November 2026
🕐 13:00 to 14:30 CET
💻 Online via Microsoft Teams

Register here!

Whether you are interested in user research, public acceptance or the implementation of new approaches to risk assessment, this webinar offers an opportunity to explore the social dimensions of building the VHP.

This webinar is part of the VHP4Safety webinar series. Join us also on 14 October for an introduction to the Virtual Human Platform and on 28 October for a deep dive into the Parkinson’s disease case study.
