To promote the collaboration between ONTOX and VHP4Safety, in 2025 we are launching a series of webinars emphasizing early-stage researchers from each of the working groups.

Kicking off the series is the Research Output Management group, already next week Friday, 14th of February from 15:00-16:30. They will be represented by Marcha Verheijen, Jente Houweling, Eliska Kuchovska and Danyel Jennen, who will speak about ToxTemp and Biostudies. Curious about what they will present?

Over the past decades the demand for open science has increased. It is required by many publishers and even mandatory under Horizon Europe. However, making your research process and output openly available can be challenging, especially the what, where and when. In this webinar, we will show BioStudies, the repository for ONTOX and VHP4Safety research output, as well as the ToxTemp format for capturing your cell-based NAMs.

BioStudies: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/

ToxTemp: https://doi.org/10.14573/altex.1909271
