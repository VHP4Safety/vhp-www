The H2020-funded NanoSolveIT project is developing a cloud platform, which implements and integrates various nanoinformatics tools for performing hazard, exposure and risk assessment studies. In this talk we will present the microservice architecture and the technical specifications of the platform, which allow hosting the different services under a common framework and addressing interoperability and scalability requirements. We will also demonstrate how the users of the platform can access and use through web-based user-friendly interfaces, various modelling tools, including QSAR and read across models, biokinetics models, image analysis services and omics analysis tools. We will end the presentation by showcasing a number of integrated applications, where several NanoSolveIT services are interconnected through APIs to perform consensus modelling and design integrated risk assessment workflows.
