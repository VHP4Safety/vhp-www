On 23 and 24 September 2024 the 3rd VHP4Safety Hackathon will take place in Utrecht. The location will be the Mezzanine room (room 2.22) at the Minnaert building at Utrecht University, Leuvenlaan 4.

The title of this Hackathon is “Large Language Models, what are they and how they can be useful for the Virtual Human Platform”. The Hackathon will be preceded by three preparatory webinars on Friday 6, 13 and 20 September from 16:00-17:00.

On the first day, we will have dinner at an external location. On the second day, the hackathon will be until 16:00, after which there will be a meeting on the user perspective for VHP4Safety researchers from 16:00-18:00. People joining this meeting can also sign up for a pizza dinner. If you have any dietary wishes, please mention this in the registration form.

Please register for this event using the following link: https://forms.uu.nl/universiteitutrecht-dgk/Hackathon_VHP4Safety.
