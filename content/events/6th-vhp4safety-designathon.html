The 6th VHP4Safety Designathon will take place on April 3 & 4 in Nieuwegein. With the final year of the project ahead, this Designathon is an important moment to reflect on our progress, align next steps, and ensure a strong finish.

This edition will focus on moving forward toward the project’s final product by jointly setting priorities. Discussions will also include planning for the final year and strategies to maximize our impact. In addition, there will be a workshop on innovation systems, exploring how innovation is shaped and implemented, particularly relevant for our co-funders.

For more information, send an email to vhp4safety@uu.nl.
