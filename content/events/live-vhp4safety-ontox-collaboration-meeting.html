Save the date for this meeting between reserachers of both projects!

We are working together with ONTOX in Collaboration Groups (previously called Task Forces). Topics are Chemical selection, Risk Assessment, Data Management, qAOP and Stakeholders involvement. We also aim to start a Collaboration Group on IT of the platforms. In these groups we aim to jointly work on topics that are relevant for both projects. On April 26th, the Collaboration Groups will present their progress and will get together to work on their plans for the coming year. If you are involved in one of these Collaboration Groups, or if you are interested to join on of the groups, please subscribe to this day. An invitation will follow early 2024.

The program will also become available early 2024.
