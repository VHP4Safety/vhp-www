We invite you to the upcoming workshop on “The Why and How of Making Your Research Output FAIR”. This workshop is highly recommended for all PhD students and ‘Postdocs’ in the consortium, as it will provide you with essential knowledge and practical tips to make your research output Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR), tailored to your specific needs.

During the workshop, you will learn what is already FAIR about your current research output, as well as how you can make it even more FAIR. By doing so, you will have the necessary knowledge to fully participate in the upcoming Designathon and contribute to the consortium’s efforts to improve the reusability and reproducibility of research output.

While attendance is not mandatory, we highly recommend that you attend this workshop. We hope you take advantage of this opportunity to enhance your FAIR Literacy.
