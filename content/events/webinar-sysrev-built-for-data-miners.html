Sysrev is an intelligent web-based platform for document reviews and data extraction. Our philosophy at Sysrev is to allow researchers to "extract" any type of data from any type of documents.

As a result, Sysrev has been used for a wide variety of document review tasks, ranging from Systematic Literature Reviews to complex data curation for machine learning applications.

This presentation will give an overview of the core Sysrev features, machine learning capabilities, the Sysrev API, and examples of Sysrev “projects" that are relevant to VHP4Safety objectives.

Dr. Thomas Luechtefeld, CEO of Sysrev, will give an interesting webinar on the possibilities of Sysrev.
