We are very pleased to celebrate the start of the NWA-ORC project the Virtual Human Platform for safety assessment, in short VHP4Safety. This event is open to the general public, but registration here is required. Please feel free to forward this invitation within your own institution and network. We hope to see you the first of June 2021.

PROGRAM
June 1st 2021, 15:00 to 16:30 CEST

Welcome
Prof. dr. Juliette Legler, Project Leader VHP4Safety

The science of communication
How can you make sure that you don’t get lost in jargon when you are working in an interdisciplinary team? What are factors that can help you to communicate about complex and sensitive issues with a broad audience? And how can the use of metaphors hinder or enable a transition like the one VHP4Safety aims for? Ionica Smeets will answer these questions with scientific studies and real-life examples and help you to communicate better within and outside The Virtual Human Platform for Safety Assessment.

Prof. dr. Ionica Smeets, Science Communication, Leiden University
Ionica Smeets is professor of science communication at Leiden University, The Netherlands. Her main research interest is bridging the gap between experts and the general public. She also practices science communication, for instance by writing columns for national newspaper De Volkskrant, presenting popular-scientific television shows and making a children’s book on mathematics.
