After five years of research and collaboration, the Virtual Human Platform for Safety Assessment (VHP4Safety) project is excited to share its final project results with the broader stakeholder community during the VHP4Safety Scientific Conference on 21 and 22 January 2027.

During these two days, we will share and discuss the scientific results of the VHP4Safety project and look ahead. What is needed to advance human-relevant safety assessment and support its wider adoption in scientific and regulatory practice? Join researchers, risk assessors, consortium partners and international peers to exchange perspectives and help shape the next steps.

Shaping the future of safety assessment

How can we advance safety assessment without relying primarily on animal testing? How do we build confidence in new human-relevant methods and move from promising science towards broader application and acceptance?

Over two days, the VHP4Safety Scientific Conference will explore these questions through:

  • Keynote presentations
  • Scientific results from VHP4Safety
  • Interactive workshops
  • In-depth scientific sessions
  • Cross-disciplinary discussion and exchange

Together, we will reflect on what has been achieved over the past five years and, most importantly, explore what comes next for human-relevant safety assessment.

Who is the conference for?

The conference is intended for professionals involved in the development, assessment or application of new approaches to safety assessment, including:

  • Researchers
  • Public-sector risk assessors
  • Industry risk assessors
  • VHP4Safety consortium partners
  • International scientific and regulatory peers
  • NGO representatives

The conference provides an opportunity to connect across disciplines, exchange scientific insights and contribute to the future development of the field.
