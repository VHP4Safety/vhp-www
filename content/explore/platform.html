The development version of the platform can be accessed via platform.vhp4safety.nl.

In the VHP4Safety project, we are developing a platform for the safety assessment of chemicals and pharmaceuticals, called the Virtual Human Platform. Although animal testing is still commonly used, its ability to predict human outcomes is limited. With this platform, we move toward animal-free safety assessment.


The platform is currently being developed using designathon, hacakthons, and the Scrum methodology. Our product goal captures what we aim to deliver by the end of the project:


“By the end of the VHP4Safety project, a sustainable* virtual human platform will have been developed. The basis of this platform is a generic risk assessment framework with tools and services, and data, integrated into a user-friendly interface, designed and tested for use by relevant stakeholders.

The platform also contains demonstrators from three relevant human case studies. These demonstrators show how to use the platform by addressing at least one regulatory question for each case study, with the goal of providing a risk assessment estimate. To this end, specific workflows have been designed, using and adapting the tools, services and data from the generic framework.”

* Sustainable: transparent, secured, documented, interoperable

The VHP combines multiple solutions developed in our project as sustainable building blocks. Those that are already available can be found here:

VHP4Safety Docs

The public documentation pages for the Virtual Human Platform for Safety Assessment can be found at https://docs.vhp4safety.nl. Here you will find cloud service descriptions and their basic tutorials. In addition, here we present example workflows that utilize two or more cloud services collaboratively to accomplish a more complex analysis. Below you can find a table with some examples of the services and workflows.

VHP4Safety Glossary

The researchers in the project developed a glossary to facilitate interdisciplinary collaboration which is available at https://glossary.vhp4safety.nl. Here we establish a common language among the project members that can be used to support the development of the VHP4Safety solutions.

VHP4Safety Cloud

Assuming that the Platform is the Virtual Human, the Cloud can be seen as a collection of organs, each providing a specific and important functionality to support risk assessment. These cloud services developed in the VHP4Ssfety project along with the external services that are relevant to risk assessment are available via our cloud: https://cloud.vhp4safety.nl.
