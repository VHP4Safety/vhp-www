Last week, we hosted the fourth VHP4Safety Hackathon, focused on "Using and expanding the first version of the VHP4Safety platform." It was an exciting event where the first user interfaces were showcased, and end users had the chance to provide valuable input on the tools in development. 🏆 Congratulations to @Linde Schoenmaker and her team, who won the hackathon with their work on a chemical hazard identification tool! This tool can help predict molecular initiating events. Curious to learn more about this tool?
