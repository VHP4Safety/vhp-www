Attending @EUROTOX Congress 2025 in Athens? Join our session "The Virtual Human in Toxicology" on Tuesday 16 September from 14:00–15:30! This session explores how virtual human platforms, such as those developed in VHP4Safety and @ONTOX, are transforming chemical safety assessment. We’ll highlight how data science and NGRA approaches enable human-relevant predictions without animal testing. Chaired by @Anne Kienhuis (RIVM) and @Chris Evelo (Maastricht University) With talks from: – @Chris Evelo (VHP4Safety) – @Holly Mortensen (EPA) – @Andrew White (Unilever) Don’t miss this forward-looking discussion on integrating tools, data, and regulatory science to build the toxicology of the future. #EUROTOX2025 #VHP4Safety #ONTOX #VirtualHuman #NGRA #Toxicology #HumanRelevant #RiskAssessment #AnimalFreeTesting
