Our coordinator @Juliette Legler presented the Utrecht Science Lecture on September 25, to mark the start of this year’s Utrecht Science Week. Her lecture entitled ‘Healthy Planet, Healthy People: Towards Solutions for a Polluted World’ outlined chemical pollution as a growing challenge to environmental and human health. Her main message: research helps us identify which pollutants matter most, understand how they affect people and the environment, and guide solutions toward a healthier planet. The format of her lecture was a surprise to the audience: a ‘mash up’ of different speakers from various disciplines who represented four main approaches towards solutions: diagnose, prioritize, innovate and predict. And, of course, VHP4Safety was part of the mix. Juliette introduced the project and @Marc Teunis from the @Hogeschool Utrecht gave the audience an outlook on the potential of AI to help better predict the health risks of chemicals. She closed by emphasising the importance of connection across disciplines, sectors, generations, cultures, backgrounds, the ideas are formed that transform our future. In this time of multiple planetary crises, she stressed that ‘connection always leads to hope.’ In the evening, Juliette also presented at the Betweter Festival.
