A festive day 🙌 . VHP4Safety's sister project #AFARA was launched today at the Utrecht University Hall. The goal of the project “Animal-Free Assays for endocrine disruption: from science to Regulatory Acceptance” (AFARA) is to facilitate the pathways for regulatory acceptance of existing models for endocrine disruption that are both animal-free and human-relevant. @Juliette Legler: “Endocrine disrupting chemicals (EDCs) are increasingly linked to various human diseases acting via the endocrine system. That's why protection of public health from the potentially harmful effects of exposure to EDCs requires human-relevant testing strategies.” “Within the project we are combining academic expertise across the natural sciences, social sciences and humanities with key public sector expertise on EDC regulation” states @Jarno Hoekman of the Copernicus Institute of Sustainable Development. “It’s fantastic that we can work together with the industry, NGOs, regulatory authorities and model developers to achieve better protection of citizens from EDC-related harm and reduce the use of animal testing.”
