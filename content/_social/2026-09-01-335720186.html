VHP4Safety at EUROTOX 2026: AI-Driven Digital Platforms for Risk Assessment On Sunday, 13 September, as part of @EUROTOX Congress 2026, VHP4Safety, @ONTOX, @eTRANSAFE, and @VICT3R will host the continuous education course: “AI-Driven Digital Platforms for Human Health Risk Assessment: Integrating OPRA and VHP in Next-Generation Approaches.” The course, led by @Marc Teunis, will explore how AI and digital infrastructures are transforming chemical risk assessment, with a focus on the OPRA and VHP platforms. Attendees will learn how these platforms support robust, transparent, and human-relevant safety assessments, reducing reliance on animal testing.
