How can AI help us to become less dependent on animal testing? Our colleague @Marc Teunis explains this to @Proefdiervrij in their recent article! At @Hogeschool Utrecht, Marc is developing AI tools to predict substance safety, reducing the reliance on animal models. His work not only enhances ethical research but also improves accuracy in toxicology. Next to this, Marc is involved in educating future professionals on the application of AI in this field, contributing to the shift towards animal-free testing.
