What will it take to move human-relevant safety assessment from promising science into wider scientific and regulatory practice?

Join us in Utrecht on 21 and 22 January 2027 for the VHP4Safety Scientific Conference!

After five years of research and collaboration, we’re bringing the scientific community together to share and discuss VHP4Safety’s results, exchange perspectives and explore what needs to happen next.

Across two days, expect thought-provoking keynotes, interesting talks from our researchers, and interactive workshops. Together, we’ll consider how to advance safety assessment while reducing reliance on animal testing, and how to build confidence in new human-relevant methods.

The conference is for researchers, public and industry risk assessors, NGOs, VHP4Safety partners and international colleagues working on new approaches to risk assessment and the transition to animal-free testing.

📅 Thursday 21 and Friday 22 January 2027
📍 Villa Jongerius, Utrecht, The Netherlands
🎟️ Attendance is free. Travel and accommodation are at your own expense.
