Last Thursday and Friday, we held the 6th VHP4Safety Designathon, an inspiring two-day event full of exchange, reflection, and forward-looking discussion. We explored priorities for the final year of the project, got a first look at the platform’s user interface, enjoyed a presentation by our Ukrainian partner Pavlo Zadorozhnii, and took part in an interesting masterclass on innovation systems. Several other sessions took place as well. We’ll share a full recap soon on the VHP4Safety website. A big thank you to @KWR Water Research Institute for hosting us in such a welcoming and beautiful venue, and of course to everyone who contributed to making this Designathon a success!
