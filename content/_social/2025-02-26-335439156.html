💡 How do endocrine-disrupting chemicals impact brain development? In our latest Meet the Crew interview, we talk to @Nathalie Dierichs, a PhD researcher in Work Package 2.4 of the VHP4Safety project. She develops in vitro models to study how chemicals can interfere with thyroid hormone-regulated brain development. This research is part of one of the three case studies feeding the Virtual Human Platform, providing crucial data to improve safety assessments based on human biology instead of animal testing.
