🔍 What does it take to move from data to decision-making in exposure-led Next Generation Risk Assessment (NGRA)? At @EUROTOX Congress 2025, @Andrew White, computational toxicologist at Unilever, will share insights from exposure-led NGRA case studies and reflect on the challenges and progress in using non-animal data, tools, and models for human-relevant safety assessment.
