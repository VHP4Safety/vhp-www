And we’re off! 🚀 Today marks the official launch of the the Virtual Human Platform at the #VHPLaunch. After a warm welcome by the VHP4Safety coordination team, consisting of @Juliette Legler, @Cyrille Krul and @Anne Kienhuis, @Fabian Wagenaars guided participants through the platform and demonstrated how it can support human relevant safety assessment of chemicals and pharmaceuticals. The discussions are now continuing in the breakout sessions, where participants can explore the platform from different angles. Later this afternoon, the event continues with the info market, offering opportunities to connect with developers, researchers and end users behind the platform. A great start to an exciting afternoon full of collaboration, innovation and exchange around animal free safety assessment and the future of regulatory science. #VHP4Safety #NAMs #Toxicology #RiskAssessment #OpenScience #TPI
