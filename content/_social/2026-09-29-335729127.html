🧫 How can co-culture models make in vitro research more representative of what happens in the body? In our latest Meet the Crew interview, we talk to @Lenya de Brouwer, a PhD researcher working in Work Packages 2.1 and 2.2 of the VHP4Safety project. Lenya studies toxicokinetics and the cellular processes that can lead to adverse outcomes. Her research includes kidney and liver co-culture models, in which proximal tubular-like cells are combined with other cell types to better represent biological processes in the body. In the interview, Lenya shares: ✅ How her first experience with cell research led her to a PhD in toxicology ✅ How co-culture models can produce different effects from other in vitro models ✅ How the Virtual Human Platform can help researchers share methods, raw data and negative results ✅ What she has learned from working with researchers from different disciplines 🔗 Read the full interview here: https://lnkd.in/eFeghJ7z #VHP4Safety #MeetTheCrew #InVitroResearch #Toxicology #NGRA #Nephrotoxicity
