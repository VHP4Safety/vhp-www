You may have heard it already: last week, the Dutch National Growth Fund committed a significant investment of 124,5 million euros to establish a state-of-the-art center for animal-free biomedical testing! This substantial investment marks a pivotal moment in advancing animal-free testing methods, enhancing the safety and efficacy of treatments while prioritizing ethical considerations and minimizing animal suffering. We commend the Netherlands for their leadership in fostering innovative solutions in biomedical research. Many people of VHP4Safety were also involved in initiating this center, including our programme coordinators @Cyrille Krul and @Anne Kienhuis, and our partner @Proefdiervrij.
