Last week researchers from #VHP4Safety gathered for the third Designathon. An interactive programme with workshops about interdisciplinary collaborations, the integration of in vitro data to in silico models for risk assessment of chemicals and addressing the expectations of stakeholders and their needs in the development of the platform.
