VHP4Safety Posters at WC13 – Even More to Discover! Not all of us could make the trip to Rio de Janeiro for @WC13 Rio - World Congress on Alternatives and Animal Use in the Life Sciences, but our work will still be on display! These VHP4Safety posters will be presented on behalf of our team members who couldn’t attend in person: - "Physicochemical properties of pesticides determines applicability of epithelial in vitro models to assess pharmacokinetic absorption parameters" - poster nr. 363 by @Alex KleinJan (presented by @Cyrille Krul) - "Development of a human iPSC-derived kidney on a chip cell model for the application of toxicity testing" - poster nr. 307 by @Lenya de Brouwer (presented by @Anja Wilmes) - "Assessing Neurodegenerative Potential In Vitro: The Importance of Timing, Duration and Frequency of Exposure" - poster nr. 237 by @Julia Meerman (presented by @Victoria de Leeuw) - "Developing a testing battery for developmental neurotoxicity mediated via disrupted thyroid hormone homeostasis" - poster nr. 236 by @Nathalie Dierichs (presented by Victoria de Leeuw) A big thanks to the colleagues stepping in to present this work.
