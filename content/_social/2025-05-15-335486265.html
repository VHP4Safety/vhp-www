Last week at the 35th annual meeting of the @Society of Environmental Toxicology and Chemistry (SETAC) in Vienna, our coordinator @Juliette Legler gave a presentation in the SETAC Special Session: Bridging the scientific and regulatory gap towards effective chemical safety assessment. She presented her work in the EU project GOLIATH and highlighted VHP4Safety as an example of how we're working to support regulatory uptake of innovative methods. The session was organised by representatives from @European Chemicals Agency, @European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), JRC and @Stockholms universitet - a valuable setting to share this perspective. #VHP4Safety #SETACVienna #ScienceToPolicy #Toxicology #AnimalFreeSafetyAssessment
