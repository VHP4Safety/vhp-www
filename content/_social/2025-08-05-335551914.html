VHP4Safety at WC13 – Meet Our Presenters in Rio! From 31 August to 4 September, the @WC13 Rio - World Congress on Alternatives and Animal Use in the Life Sciences will take place in Rio de Janeiro, and VHP4Safety will be there! Several of our colleagues will be presenting their work in person. Come find them and learn more about their contributions to advancing animal-free safety assessment: - @Cyrille Krul (poster nr. 417): "Transforming Safety Assessment: the VHP4Safety Project Interdisciplinary Approach" - @Filippo Di Tillio (poster nr. 164): "Quantitative adverse outcome pathway modeling of Platinum-Induced Nephrotoxicity: Developing In Vitro and In Vivo Models for Predictive Extrapolation" - @Kiri Romano (poster nr. 241): "The Integration of In Vitro Cell-Based and Computational Approaches to Improve the Toxicological Hazard Assessment of Nephrotoxicants" - @M. (Marianne) Bol-Schoenmakers (poster nr. 371): "Promoting Interdisciplinary Education to Facilitate the Acceptance of Animal-free Methods" Looking forward to insightful discussions and new connections.
