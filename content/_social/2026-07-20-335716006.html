VHP4Safety at ESTIV 2026: A Week of Science, Collaboration and Exchange A few weeks ago, the 23rd Congress of the @European Society of Toxicology in Vitro ESTIV brought together researchers from across the field of next-generation safety assessment. VHP4Safety had a strong presence throughout the event, contributing to scientific sessions, workshops, and discussions. One of the highlights was our dedicated VHP4Safety session, where @Anne Kienhuis, @Marvin Martens, @Devon Barnes and @Isaac Arturo Ortega Alvarado demonstrated how all of our research comes together in the Virtual Human Platform. (https://lnkd.in/ed-X9Cpe) Our colleagues also contributed to many other sessions during the congress and the post-congress ASPIS Open Symposium.
