🎭 How does the public feel about new safety testing methods? Last week, students from the University of the Arts Utrecht (@HKU) explored this question in a unique way. They created two audio fragments, one representing in vivo testing and the other in vitro methods, and asked participants to listen and shape their emotions into clay figures. What emotions surfaced? What ethical dilemmas stood out? This creative approach gave us fresh insights into how the wider public perceives our work in safety testing. A fascinating and valuable perspective! #ScienceCommunication #PublicPerception #SafetyTesting #RiskAssessment #ArtAndScience #VHP4Safety
