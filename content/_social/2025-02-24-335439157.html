On Friday, 14 February, we hosted the first @ONTOX-VHP4Safety Webinar on Research Output Management. The session introduced the BioStudies database, which archives biological study descriptions and data, and ToxTemp, a template for documenting cell-based toxicological test methods for regulatory evaluation. Our PhD candidate @Jente H. presented her work on ToxTempAssistant, an innovative tool that leverages large language models to simplify and accelerate the completion of ToxTemp templates. Missed it? Watch the recording on the ASPIS YouTube channel: https://lnkd.in/e5NiFKEa. 📢 Stay tuned for our next webinar, which will focus on stakeholder involvement!
