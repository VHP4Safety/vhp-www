How do you build a sustainable, user-friendly platform that integrates toxicology data, models, and workflows - and make it usable across sectors? At @EUROTOX Congress 2025, @Chris Evelo will present how the VHP4Safety project is tackling this challenge. His talk will explore the practical side of developing the Virtual Human Platform: from agile team science and open standards to structuring platform components for real-world risk assessment. This presentation is part of the session “The Virtual Human in Toxicology”, co-organised by VHP4Safety and @ONTOX. 📍 Join us in Athens to find out how we’re building towards a more integrated, reproducible, and animal-free future in toxicology. #EUROTOX2025 #VHP4Safety #ONTOX #NAMs #Toxicology #OpenScience #FAIRdata #RiskAssessment #InterdisciplinaryResearch
