Shifting from test animals to human data as the foundation for safety assessments! Currently, many test animals are still used to assess the safety of certain substances—a practice that is regrettable, especially considering the extensive knowledge we already possess about how the human body functions. Using this existing knowledge and AI, it's possible to simulate many processes in the human body using a digital twin, eliminating the need for animal testing. Explore further insights on this groundbreaking technique in this story from @Hogeschool Utrecht!
