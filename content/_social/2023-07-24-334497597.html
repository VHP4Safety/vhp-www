Meet the Crew: get to know PhD student Julia Meerman! Every month a VHP4Safety colleague introduces themselves and shares their thoughts on our project’s ambitions and impact. This time we get to know @Julia Meerman, who is involved in work package 2.3, which focuses on Parkinson's disease and pesticides. We spoke with Julia about her interests in this project, her newly published paper and about her plans for the future. Julia: “(…) we suggest longer-term solutions that are based on human biology. This is, for instance, the approach that VHP4Safety also has, in which we first outline the human biology underlying an adverse effect and develop a kind of test battery on that foundation. This will eventually allow us to do a better assessment of these pesticides.” Want to know more? Read the full interview here: https://lnkd.in/e9K3KxFh #VHP4Safety #meetthecrew #toxicology
