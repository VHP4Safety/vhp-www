This morning at @SURF, we welcomed the stakeholders of VHP4Safety. Together we discussed how to optimise stakeholder engagement within the project. As such, we talked about collaborations in the field of data sciences, implementation, education/training and embedding the platform. The VHP4Safety consortium consists of leading scientific groups from Dutch universities, university medical centres, public health institutes and applied research organisations, with expertise spanning the technological, biological, chemical, medical as well as the social sciences. The co-funders and cooperation partners ensure the active involvement of diverse academic, regulatory, industrial and societal partners to the project throughout the entire safety assessment knowledge chain. #VHP4Safety #safetyassessment #stakeholderengagement
