Sign up for the new 1-week course on New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) in Toxicology! This course is developed by Utrecht University, RIVM and Wageningen University, in collaboration with Postgraduate Education in Toxicology (PET). This course will provide insight into how NAMs, including in vitro and computational tools, deliver a much needed mechanistic understanding of toxicity and how they can be integrated into risk assessment. After following this course, you will be able to: * choose relevant methodologies to assess the risk of chemicals including drugs, cosmetics, industrial chemicals and contaminants; * understand the opportunities and hurdles of integrating NAMs in regulatory toxicology; * appreciate the ethical and sociological drivers behind a paradigm shift towards the use of NAMs in risk assessment; * apply NAMs in your own field of work. The first edition is scheduled from 11-15 December 2023 at Wageningen University. More information: https://lnkd.in/ekPpEXBr
