🚀 Kicking Off Our ONTOX & VHP4Safety Webinar Series! To strengthen the collaboration between @ONTOX and VHP4Safety, we are launching a webinar series in 2025, highlighting early-stage researchers from each working group. First up: The Research Output Management group, on Friday, 14 February, from 15:00-16:30 CET. This session will feature @Marcha Verheijen, @Jente H., @Eliška Kuchovská, and @Danyel Jennen, who will speak about ToxTemp and BioStudies. Curious about what they will present? "Over the past decades the demand for open science has increased. It is required by many publishers and even mandatory under Horizon Europe. However, making your research process and output openly available can be challenging, especially the what, where and when.
