Heading to @EUROTOX Congress 2025 in Athens? Don’t miss the VHP4Safety poster by @Julia Meerman: "Quantifying an Adverse Outcome Pathway for Parkinson’s Disease: Putting Theory into Practice." This poster presents the first steps in quantifying (part of) an AOP network for pesticide-induced Parkinson’s disease. It highlights that the sequence of early Key Events is particularly challenging to capture in vitro - an important consideration when applying qAOPs in the safety assessment of pesticides. 📍 Visit the VHP4Safety posters at EUROTOX to explore more on advancing human-relevant, animal-free safety science. #VHP4Safety #EUROTOX2025 #qAOP #AOP #ParkinsonsDisease #Neurotoxicity #Toxicology #Neurodegeneration #AnimalFreeTesting
