As the days become shorter and the Christmas lights appear in the streets, it is time for us to look back at the past year. 2022 started digitally. But this did not stop us from organizing our first Designathon in February. Together we faced the challenge of integrating three diverse research lines, and the creation of several Task Forces was the result. The second Designathon took place in the autumn, this time in real life! Many of us came together in Maastricht and worked on AOPs, workflows and beautiful metaphors. And last but definitely not least we invited our stakeholders to the very first VHP4Safety Stakeholders Meeting. Here we presented the progress of the project and the interesting interactive discussions with several WP-leaders, researchers and stakeholders contributed to more active engagement of all participants in the project.

But besides these highlights, this year was also marked by many meetings, brainstorm sessions, discussions, progress talks, the welcoming of new colleagues, presentations at several congresses, like ICT-2022, NUTRIM2022, ESTIV and EUSAAT, mail traffic, newsletters, and much more. Therefore, we may conclude that we have had a busy year and made very exciting steps towards a safety assessment based on human data. Many thanks for your dedication and contribution. We look forward to continuing the great collaboration in 2023 and hope to see you all in more live meetings.

We wish you Happy Holidays and a wonderful New Year!

All the best,

Anne, Cyrille, Esmeralda and Juliette
