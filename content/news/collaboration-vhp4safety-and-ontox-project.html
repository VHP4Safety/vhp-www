In the project proposal of VHP4Safety we proposed to connect to other international initiatives on advancing risk assessment without the use of animals. We are happy to inform you that VHP4Safety started a collaboration with the ONTOX project. ONTOX is an EU Horizon 2020 funded project involving 18 European and US teams working on new approach methodologies to replace animal experimentation for chemical toxicity testing purposes. Like, VHP4Safety, the project started in 2021 and some of the partners involved in VHP4Safety also have a role in ONTOX.

On October 17th researchers from our project and from ONTOX had a first workshop to explore the options for collaboration. Several topics on which we can collaborate are identified, like Communication and Dissemination, Stakeholder Engagement and Sustainability. As Data Management and AOP development are important topics for both projects and following a comparable course on these topics will improve sustainability, researchers from ONTOX will join the Task Forces on AOP Development and Data Management. Two new joined Task Forces, on Risk Assessment and Chemical Selection, will make a start in 2023.

With this collaboration we hope to also connect young researchers from both project and let them participate in joined activities. Courses, workshops and webinars of both projects will be made accessible for all of us. Last, the coordination teams of both projects will explore possibilities to work together on securing future funding of our platforms.

We look forward on a good collaboration. If you, or your work package, see other options for collaboration between the two projects, let us know via vhp4safety@uu.nl!
