Last month, Frank Hepkema started as scrum master/product owner for VHP4Safety. He will mainly be collaborating with Research Line 1, focused on building the platform. Please read his introduction below!

Hi, my name is Frank Hepkema and since 18 November the scrum master/product owner for VHP4S. This means that I will help the project to adapt some agile ways of working to deliver the VHP. I expect that my long-term experience as a product owner and agile coach in many different organisations will help us within VHP4S. I am pragmatic and like to experiment with new ways of working without loosing focus of the tasks at hand.

If you have any questions regarding agile and/or scrum, please do not hesitate to contact me via frank.hepkema@hu.nl.
