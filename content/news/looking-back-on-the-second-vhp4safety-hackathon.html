On February 5th and 6th, the second VHP4Safety Hackathon was held at Utrecht University, meticulously organized by Egon, Marc, and Juliette. This hackathon was specifically focused on bridging the gap between Research Line 2, where experimental data is generated, and Research Line 1, where the platform is constructed. Effective communication between these two research lines is essential for the development of a resilient Virtual Human Platform.

The event kicked off with an introduction by Egon, outlining the agenda and emphasizing the importance of utilizing templates to facilitate effective data sharing and cocreation of the Virtual Human Platform. This introductory session was complemented by insightful talks from three experts. Danyel Jennen elucidated on ISA templates and EBI repositories, followed by Andrew White from Unilever who shared how they deal with data management at Unilever. Additionally, Nina Jealizkova provided a comprehensive guide on utilizing the eNanoMapper template wizard, equipping the teams with essential tools for the ensuing Hackathon activities. Following a satisfying lunch, five teams were formed, launching into hacking sessions for the remainder of the afternoon. The first day culminated in a delightful pizza dinner, fostering camaraderie among participants.

The second day commenced with pitch presentations from all five hackathon groups. Most groups delved into exploring the potential of templates for facilitating cohesive data communication within the project. Subsequently, the day was dedicated to further hacking endeavors, with another enjoyable lunch at midday. As the hackathon drew to a close, teams presented their progress once more, followed by the jury—comprising Danyel, Nina, and Jente—retreating for deliberation. Meanwhile, Ivo proposed an intriguing idea for the next hackathon, centered on the integration of Artificial Intelligence within the Virtual Human Platform. The event concluded with the jury announcing the winning team. Congratulations to The Neurodegenerative Pesticides for winning the VHP4Safety Challenge Cup!
