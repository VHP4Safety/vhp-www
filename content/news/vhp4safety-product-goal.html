The VHP4Safety product goal has been defined as follows:

“By the end of the VHP4Safety project, a sustainable* virtual human platform will have been developed. The basis of this platform is a generic risk assessment framework with tools and services, and data, integrated into a user-friendly interface, designed and tested for use by relevant stakeholders.
The platform also contains demonstrators from three human relevant case studies. These demonstrators show how to use the platform by addressing at least one regulatory question for each case study, with the goal of providing a risk assessment estimate. To this end, specific workflows have been designed, using and adapting the tools, services and data from the generic framework.”

* Sustainable: transparent, secured, documented, interoperable

At the start of the project, it wasn't clear what the platform would ultimately look like, but now that that's become clearer, we can use this product goal to apply more focus to our work.

For any questions about the product goal, please send an email to vhp4safety@uu.nl.
