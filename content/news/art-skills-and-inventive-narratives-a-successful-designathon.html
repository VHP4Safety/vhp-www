With great success, VHP4Safety's second Designathon took place in Maastricht a few weeks ago. We look back on all the wonderful meetings and inspiring sessions that were organised. Be sure to check out our team page for photos, recordings and presentations. One of the sessions organised during the Designathon was about creating a common vision for the platform. Marjolein Hoogstraaten, researcher at Copernicus Institute of Sustainable Development, looks back on this session and tells more about her experiences.

A common understanding

We started the second day of the second designathon with a session focused on the creation of a common vision of the VHP4Safety platform. A session that was in essence a bit different and perhaps more playful than the other sessions, nonetheless with serious ambitions. The first day of the designathon, which included an in-depth session on AOPs, already brough to light that our diverse platform members may not yet have a precise or common vision on what the VHP4Safety platform is or should be able to deliver. The session on creating a common vision was aimed specifically at increasing common understanding of what our platform is and what we want it to do, while simultaneously understanding better how others within the consortium view the platform.

We used metaphors to be able to communicate in a different way than we as scientists usually do and to strive to move away from our jargon and find common language. As a first exercise, bureau EMMA that guided this session, provided us with association cards, challenging us to describe the previous day using one of these cards. This exercise helped us to think and communicate through visual aids and metaphors and paved the way for the rest of the session, which was focused on challenging different groups to come up with a metaphor that described the VHP4Safety platform. All groups enthusiastically got to work, creative art skills were unlocked and inventive narratives were articulated. Eventually this resulted in eight posters showcasing different metaphors. The metaphors ranged from a 3D printed crash test dummy to a spiderweb umbrella, with traffic lights, other planets and mountain climbers in between.

So, what are now our next steps? We will analyze the metaphors that the consortium came up with and distill themes and concepts that are commonly viewed as useful to describe the VHP4Safety platform. This brings us a step closer towards the design of an animated video about our platform in the future. In the end we look back on a fun and useful session that has increased mutual understanding amongst the consortium members and their views on the platform and that will eventually help us to shape our external communication to the broader public.
