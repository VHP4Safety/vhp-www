Hi all, my name is Fabian Wagenaars and since April 15th I have been working as a postdoc on the VHP4Safety project. In my role as postdoc, I will focus on integrating the different models, tools and datasets developed in the VHP4Safety project into work/process flows in order to answer regulatory questions for each of the case studies. Moreover, I will aid in bridging the gap between RL1 and RL2 and help in bringing the work/process flows and case studies onto the virtual human platform. I am excited to connect different expertise groups together and utilize my own experience as a scientific researcher onto the project.
