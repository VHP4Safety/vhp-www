On Tuesday 17 and Wednesday 18 March, the 8th VHP4Safety Hackathon was held. As the final hackathon before the platform’s official launch, the focus was on completing the remaining tasks to ensure all necessary components were integrated and functional.

The event began with an introductory presentation, where participants were divided into three working groups, each assigned specific tasks:

  • Group 1 (Experimental Data & SOPs) – Led by Jente Houweling, this group worked on annotating and uploading datasets and metadata to repositories such as BioStudies and Zenodo. They also created an inventory of research outputs and used the ToxTempAssistant tool to populate ToxTemps.

  • Group 2 (Models & Metadata) – Under the leadership of Abhishek Chattopadhyay, the team focused on using the Research Object Builder for Open Toxicology (ROBOT), a web-based application designed for creating, validating, and depositing FAIR-compliant toxicology model metadata in the form of RO-Crates.

  • Group 3 (Project & RL3 Information) – Led by Isaac Ortega Alvarado, this group worked on integrating project-related information into the platform. To avoid cluttering the risk assessment interface, they decided to use a hamburger menu for this content. The team drafted the necessary text and created supporting images to accompany it.

In parallel, two tool developers recorded introductory videos for tutorials, which will be made available to users as part of the platform’s documentation.

At the end of the hackathon, a vote was held - after all, it was election day - to determine the most productive group. Group 1 was declared the winner, earning recognition for their contributions.

The results of this hackathon will be finalized in the coming weeks as the platform prepares for its official release. Updates on the launch will follow soon.
