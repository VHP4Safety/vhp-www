The first day opened with an introduction by Egon, who kicked things off with an interactive exercise to help define the hackathon themes. Participants first formed a line based on the end date of their contracts, which created a visual overview of whose work should be prioritised for integration into the platform. In the second part of the exercise, participants paired up to exchange ideas on what should happen next for the platform, followed by pitching their partner’s idea to the group.

Using these ideas, four groups were formed:

• Group 1 worked on the case study documentation

• Group 2 focused on completing documentation for specific tools

• Group 3 identified gaps in the tool documentation

• Group 4 worked on improving the tools pages

Once the groups were set, the hacking began. After lunch in the hospital restaurant, work continued throughout the afternoon, and the day concluded with intermediate presentations from all groups.

On the first day, Group 1 focused on including bioassays on the platform. As documenting these bioassays can be time consuming, the group explored ways to streamline this process using a large language model. This resulted in the creation of the VHP A NOTEtator.

Group 2 worked on documenting their tools. Building on existing examples, they developed a documentation format that is now available via the VHP4Safety documentation website: Documentation Format — VHP4Safety-Docs 0.0.1.9000 documentation. This format was used to write new documentation. In addition, the group worked on integrating the glossary into the platform, allowing definitions to appear when users hover over specific terms. They also started developing scripts for tutorial videos for the AOP Suite tool and the QSPRpred tool.

Group 3 created a spreadsheet overview of tools that are already on the platform and those that still need to be added. For each tool, they identified which information is required and what is currently missing.

Group 4 focused on optimising the Tools and Process Flow pages, including making several textual improvements.

In the evening, participants enjoyed dinner at a lovely Italian restaurant in the Maastricht city centre, followed by a visit to the Magical Maastricht Christmas market, a festive end to a productive first day.

The second day started with more focused hacking, again followed by lunch in the hospital restaurant. In the afternoon, all groups worked towards their final presentations.

Group 1 continued working on adding bioassays to the platform. As the VHP A NOTEtator was not available on the second day, this work was completed manually. Additionally, they changed the layout and the content on the case study pages on the platform. These can now be found under the “Methods” section of the platform website.

Group 2 finalised documentation for their tools, completed the script for the QSPRpred tutorial video, recorded the tutorial video, and added the “Methods” section to the platform where the bioassays are presented.

Group 3 examined how the different tools are linked to the Process Flow steps and to the case studies. They also worked on identifying contact persons for each tool so this information can be added to the individual tool pages.

Group 4 continued optimising the individual tool pages by adding content to the various tabs and reviewing navigation on the main tools overview page.

The complete presentations from the hackathon are available on the VHP4Safety Teams.
