Did you miss the launch of the Virtual Human Platform (VHP), or would you like to learn more about the topics discussed during the different sessions? This autumn, VHP4Safety is organising a three-part webinar series introducing the Virtual Human Platform and showing how it can be used in human-relevant, animal-free safety assessment.

The webinars will provide an opportunity to explore the platform, its tools and the research behind its development.

Introduction to the VHP
14 October 2026, 13:00 to 14:30 CEST

The first webinar will introduce the VHP4Safety project and the Virtual Human Platform. Participants will see how the platform works, explore its tools and learn how it can support safety assessment workflows. The programme will include a live demonstration of the platform and an example of how it was used to address a regulatory question related to thyroid hormone-mediated brain development.

Registration here for the first webinar.

Deep dive into the Parkinson's case study
28 October 2026, 13:00 to 14:30 CET

The second webinar will focus on VHP4Safety’s Parkinson’s case study. Speakers will explain how the Virtual Human Platform brings together in vitro research, Adverse Outcome Pathways, experimental data and available methods to support risk assessment, as demonstrated through the Parkinson’s case study.

Register here for the second webinar.

From innovation to implementation
18 November 2026, 13:00 to 14:30 CET

The final webinar of the VHP4Safety webinar series will focus on the social side of building the VHP, including user research supporting the development and implementation of the Virtual Human Platform. It will also examine the public acceptance of computational models and artificial intelligence in risk assessment. The webinar will show how stakeholder needs and perspectives can support the development of tools that are scientifically robust, transparent and fit for purpose.

Register here for the third and final webinar.

All webinars will take place online via Microsoft Teams, from 13:00 to 14:30. Whether you missed the launch or want to explore the Virtual Human Platform in more depth, the series offers an opportunity to learn more and get started.
