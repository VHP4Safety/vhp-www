From 8-11 September, the annual EUROTOX Congress took place in Copenhagen, bringing together leading experts in toxicology from across Europe. It was an exciting event for VHP4Safety, with several key members of the team receiving prestigious awards and recognition.

Juliette Legler, VHP4Safety coordinator, was awarded the EUROTOX 2024 Lecture Award, a high honor presented to scientists who have made outstanding contributions to the science of drug and chemical toxicology. In her lecture, Juliette discussed endocrine disruptors and emphasized the importance of forging connections between disciplines and stakeholders to overcome complex scientific challenges. Her work highlights the need for collaboration in tackling toxicological issues.

In addition, Nathalie Dierichs was recognized with the 2024 ECETOC Christa Hennes Award: Highly Commended Recognition for her fascinating presentation on her new findings on the development of a human-based testing battery for assessing developmental neurotoxicity, mediated by disruption of thyroid hormone homeostasis. Her research represents a significant step forward in creating non-animal testing alternatives for chemical safety.

The VHP4Safety team was further celebrated with Ivonne Rietjens winning the EUROTOX Merit Award, honoring her distinguished career and lasting contributions to European toxicology.

We are immensely proud of our team’s achievements at EUROTOX 2024, which underscore the groundbreaking work being done in toxicology and human-based safety assessment within the VHP4Safety project.
