On November 10 and 11, the 7th VHP4Safety Designathon took place in Utrecht. The meeting opened on Monday afternoon with a shared lunch, during which the posters were set up for viewing. After lunch, Fabian Wagenaars gave an update on the current state of the platform, followed by breakout discussions in which groups reviewed and commented on individual platform pages. By rotating between tables, all participants gained a thorough overview of the latest developments. The session concluded with poster pitches and an extended break to browse the posters in more detail.

After the break, the group reconvened for a second platform session, this time focused on priorities. With the project entering its final phase, participants discussed which developments should be emphasised to maximise usability and stimulate future adoption. The conversations generated valuable insights into the decisions that will shape the platform in the coming months.

The day ended with a joint dinner at Het Oude Tolhuys, offering a welcome moment to unwind and continue discussions in an informal setting.

The second day began with a talk by Iryna Popova from Bukovinian State Medical University in Ukraine. She presented her research on how stressors such as iodine deficiency and disrupted circadian rhythm, exacerbated by the ongoing war, affect thyroid function. Her presentation sparked considerable interest and discussion among the participants.

The morning continued with a “Sustainathon”, where after an introductory presentation by Isaac Ortega Alvarado, participants explored how different components of the platform could be sustained after the project concludes. The session included reflections on legal, technical, and social aspects that will play a role in long-term platform continuity. This work will continue in the coming period as the consortium shapes its sustainability strategy.

Following lunch, all work packages provided updates on their progress over the past year and shared their plans for the remainder of the project. The Designathon closed with a final wrap-up and informal drinks, offering space to exchange experiences and reflect on the two days of collaborative work.

The 7th Designathon was a productive and engaging meeting, and we look forward to continuing this momentum at the next project gathering.
