A virtual human instead of laboratory animals for testing chemicals and medicines, doesn’t that sound interesting? That is exactly what we are trying to achieve here at VHP4Safety. In an interview with 'Chemie Magazine', Juliette Legler, Cyrille Krul and Anne Kienhuis give insight into our project. Our goals, our research process and the different challenges that come with it are discussed. “We think that we can build a system that is more reliable (for the testing of chemicals and medicines) than what we have now” states Legler.
