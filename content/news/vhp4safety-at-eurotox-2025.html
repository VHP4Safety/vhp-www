This year’s EUROTOX Congress in Athens featured a strong presence from the VHP4Safety consortium. Multiple posters and two dedicated scientific sessions highlighted the project’s progress and its role in driving forward next-generation, animal-free safety assessment.


Anne Kienhuis and Chris Evelo hosted the session The Virtual Human in Toxicology, bringing together three expert speakers to explore how in silico tools, the Adverse Outcome Pathway (AOP) framework, and exposure-led case studies contribute to human-relevant risk assessment. Chris Evelo opened the session with a presentation on how the VHP4Safety platform is being developed from software and data, integrating reproducible research and predictive modelling. Holly Mortensen (EPA) followed with an overview of new approaches to data and model integration in the US EPA’s AOP database. Andrew White concluded the session with a presentation on the utility of exposure-led NGRA case studies, highlighting both progress and challenges in integrating non-animal data and models for regulatory use.


In the thyroid hormone-related neurodevelopmental toxicity session hosted by Ellen Hessel, she presented the project’s case study on thyroid hormone-related neurodevelopmental toxicity. Her talk addressed how TH deficiencies during pregnancy can disrupt brain development, potentially leading to neurodevelopmental disorders in children. Ellen underscored the need for brain-specific New Approach Methodologies (NAMs), given current assays’ limitations. She also introduced an AOP framework and network designed to support the development of such methods and improve risk assessment and chemical regulation.


Alongside these sessions, several VHP4Safety researchers presented posters across a wide range of topics, showcasing the project’s interdisciplinary efforts and collaborative results. The congress provided a great opportunity to connect with the broader toxicology community and share how VHP4Safety is contributing to the transition toward animal-free safety assessment.
