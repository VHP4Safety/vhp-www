On World Day for Laboratory Animals, we’re proud to share the official VHP4Safety project video!


This video gives an inside look at how researchers, developers and societal partners across the VHP4Safety consortium are working together to build the Virtual Human Platform — a new framework for animal-free, human-relevant safety assessment of chemicals and pharmaceuticals.


Curious how science, technology and society come together in this interdisciplinary project?


Watch the video below to learn more about our shared ambition.

VHP4Safety - the Virtual Human Platform for safety assessment - is a research project funded by the Dutch Research Council (NWO) program entitled the ‘Dutch Research Agenda: Research on Routes by Consortia (NWA-ORC).’ With a budget of over 10 million Euros, the project started on June 1, 2021 and will last for the duration of 5 years.
