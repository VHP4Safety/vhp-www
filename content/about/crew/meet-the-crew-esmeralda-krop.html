Welcome, Esmeralda! Tell us a bit about yourself.
My name is Esmeralda Krop, I studied Medical Biology and I have a PhD in Immunology. For this PhD, I studied the development of allergies in people that work with laboratory animals. Reducing the use of laboratory animals will also reduce the prevalence of these allergies so there's a bit of a connection with the aims of VHP4Safety.

After my PhD, I started working at IRAS, the Institute for Risk Assessment Sciences at Utrecht University, where I learned a lot about epidemiology. At IRAS, I found out that I really like research project management. The job is highly varied, from arranging contracts and agreements to connecting people. I've been working as a project manager for 6 years now and am still enjoying it a lot.

What made you choose this project?
I really like connecting people, expertises and skills. Basically, finding out where we need to build bridges and then making that happen. That's what drew me to this project in particular. It's highly complex: so many disciplines, different partners and points of view. There are lots of cross-connections between the work packages. We need all of this to work well together if we want to achieve our aims. I'm really keen to help make that a reality.

What will be your first steps in this new role?
My first aim will be to really understand the project. I'm planning meetings with all WP leaders to get to know them, understand their work packages and understand how they want to achieve their goals. I hope this will give me a good overview, so I can provide the best possible support.

From the talks I've had so far, I see a lot of enthusiastic people with a lot of skills, and they are making great progress in the project. But I also see that it’s not always easy for people to find other experts working within the project. I hope to be able to help with this as well.

And what do you do when you're not busy ensuring projects run smoothly?
I'm a single mother to a lovely 10-year-old daughter, so I spend a lot of time with her. I enjoy going on long walks with her or with friends, and I like to read a lot too. I'm quite creative and I enjoy hand lettering, painting and drawing. And then I also like skiing and I do that once or twice a year. So many hobbies, so little time!

Do you have a question for Esmeralda? Contact her on vhp4safety@uu.nl
