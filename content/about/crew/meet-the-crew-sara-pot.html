A new team member. Welcome Sara! Tell us a bit about yourself.
My name is Sara Pot, I am 28 years old and live in the center of the most beautiful city in the Netherlands: Utrecht. I studied at Utrecht University and got my bachelor's degree in Educational Sciences and after that I finished my master's at the Utrecht University School of Governance. With great pleasure I decided to stay on at UU after my studies. The past 4.5 years I worked as a project coordinator and relationship manager for the team Alumni Relations and the Utrecht University Fund. Over the past few years, I have rolled out various projects from start to finish and I have been involved in all aspects of these. From involving new partners, to event organization and communication.

It has always been my wish to make the switch to a faculty one day. I therefore find it very special that I am allowed to work for the center of expertise and point of reference for veterinary issues for the entire country, and increasingly for the world abroad as well.

And what will you be doing for VHP4Safety?
I am responsible for the communication of this research project. I focus on internal community building, stakeholder engagement and media outreach. But… before I can do this properly, my aim will be to understand the project.

That’s why I am now reading up on the project and all the proposals, protocols and project documents that have been written. Very interesting so far, a new world is opening up to me. Hopefully, with my broad view from outside the field of work and creativity, I can help to ensure that the project will be adequately communicated.

Is there anything specific you are really looking forward to working on within the project?
What I particularly like about this project is that it involves a lot of different actors and stakeholders from all kinds of backgrounds and disciplines. I am really looking forward to working with them on this and seeing how we can keep everyone engaged in the project in the right way. Definitely a long-term challenge. So then also something I'll be working on in the short term: the website for VHP4Safety. This will be updated and expanded a little further. Hopefully this site will be a great representation of everything everyone is working so hard on within the consortium.

Summer is over and the new academic year has started again. Do you have any good resolutions for this year?
Hmm that's a good question. Maybe making sure my bag doesn't explode because of all the papers (i.e., pencils, tea bags, biscuits, fruit, notebooks, etc. etc.) I collect in it. Let's aim for a clean bag policy this new academic year.
