  • Work Package 2.1: In vitro models to provide toxicokinetic parameters, to identify the appropriate human in vitro models based on well-defined exposures with model compounds, as well as compounds selected in the case studies. WP leader: Raymond Pieters.
  • Work Package 2.2: Disease scenario: case study ‘kidney disease’, to study disease and pharmacovigilance. WP leader: Roos Masereeuw.
  • Work Package 2.3: Life course exposure scenario: case study ‘life course pesticide exposure and neurodegenerative disease’, to study life course exposure and neurodegenerative disease (i.e., Parkinson’s disease). WP leader: Jacqueline van Engelen.
  • Work Package 2.4: Age and sex scenario: case study ‘thyroid mediated developmental neurotoxicity’, to study health effects discriminated by age and sex on thyroid mediated neurodevelopment. WP leader: Ellen Hessel.
