  • Work Package 1.1: Building the ICT infrastructure, to establish the foundations of the VHP based on the needs and requirements of the scientists and end-users (industry and regulators). WP leader: Egon Willighagen.
  • Work Package 1.2: In silico toxicokinetics, to collect and improve in silico tools to enable the translation of in vitro data obtained in human relevant bioassays to the in vivo situation. WP leader: Nynke Kramer.
  • Work Package 1.3: Computational toxicodynamics, to employ and integrate key in silico tools, including artificial intelligence, or machine learning, structure-based modelling, bioinformatics, and quantitative Adverse Outcome Pathway (qAOP) models. WP leaders: Chris Evelo and Marvin Martens.
